The problem

Most feedback programmes measure, then stop.

Scores get collected, dashboards get built, and very little changes for the customer who complained.

The score without the reason

A number tells you something is wrong. It does not tell you which journey, which release or which policy caused it.

Feedback sits in its own silo

Survey data disconnected from the customer profile cannot trigger a journey, inform an offer or reach the frontline.

The loop never closes

By the time a detractor case reaches someone empowered to fix it, the customer has already moved on.

How it works

From a single response to a resolved case.

Every stage is designed to reduce the time between a customer speaking and someone acting.

STEP 01

Capture

Surveys are triggered by real events across web, app, email and messaging, sized to the moment rather than the calendar.

STEP 02

Unify

Responses are stitched to the customer profile, so a score carries the full context of who gave it and what preceded it.

STEP 03

Interpret

AI classifies themes and sentiment across open text, surfacing emerging issues before they show up in the headline number.

STEP 04

Route

Detractor and at-risk cases are pushed to the owning team with the context needed to act, in minutes rather than reporting cycles.

STEP 05

Close

Recovery journeys run automatically, with resolution and follow-up tracked back against the original response.

STEP 06

Measure

Journey and channel level reporting shows which fixes moved the score and which did not.
